    Why hybrid? — the architecture, on one page

    TaskRunsWhy
    Instant Q&A, flashcards, short summaries, short translationOn-device · Gemini NanoLatency (ms, not seconds), privacy (a child's question never leaves the device), $0 marginal cost, works on a bus with no signal.
    Worked solutions, mock tests, photo problems, long passagesCloud · Gemini Flash → ProMulti-step reasoning depth a small on-device model should not fake. The cascade escalates only when confidence demands it — and shows the hand-off.
    Deep task while offlineLocal helper now + cloud catch-upAnswered immediately from the on-device lesson bank, queued transparently, and re-run against the cloud the moment connectivity returns.
    History, progress, cached lessonsDevice only, by defaultSync is opt-in, never silent. Children's learning data is not a cloud default.

    Every answer in this app carries its routing decision — tier, model, reason, latency, estimated cost. Hybrid inference is not a hidden optimisation here; it is the product surface, because a student (and a judge) should be able to see why an answer came from where it did.
